The Story

Ellie and the Kronogns started as a half-serious porch project and accidentally became something with a name, a sound, and a suspicious amount of emotional sincerity.

The Sound

Acoustic-first songs with folk, indie, and beach-house energy. Think handwritten notes, warm strings, late-night jokes, and melodies that refuse to leave.

The Debut

The first album, Sweet Baby Jake Goes to Sandy Toes, captures the spirit of family chaos, vacation weather, and little memories that somehow become anthems.

The Vibe

Semi-professional, fully unserious when needed, and always trying to sound like a real band even when the dog is walking through the recording.
